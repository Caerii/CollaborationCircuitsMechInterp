# Experiment 12: Information-Theoretic Analysis

## Goal
Ground our findings in rigorous information theory:
- Mutual Information: I(activations; agent_belief)
- Conditional MI: I(activations; belief | context)
- Transfer Entropy: Information flow between layers

## Methods
1. Mutual Information estimation (KSG estimator)
2. Information bottleneck analysis
3. Redundancy/synergy decomposition (PID)

## Why This Matters
- Probes can overfit; MI is more principled
- Quantifies how much information is preserved/lost
- Identifies information bottlenecks in the circuit
